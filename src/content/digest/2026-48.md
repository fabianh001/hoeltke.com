This week brings two mathematics results that will take time to check, a founder returning to run Nvidia, a small model built for local hardware, and a projection of what will generate Anthropic's electricity in 2028.

## Nvidia's board brings Jensen Huang back as CEO

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO, and a board director on November 23. The founder holds all three roles again, according to the company's announcement. Nvidia designs the GPUs most large models are trained and served on.

**Why it matters:** Nvidia's roadmap and allocation decisions set how much AI compute is available and roughly what it costs, so who runs the company is a supply question for everyone who rents or buys GPUs. Whether the reinstatement means continuity with the existing strategy or a course change is the part customers will be watching. ([Jensen Huang Reinstated as Nvidia CEO](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director))

## A Lean-checked proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

A proof of the Riemann Hypothesis has been formalized in Lean, a proof assistant that checks each step against a small trusted kernel. The result reportedly arrives alongside a second, independent proof. The hypothesis, open since 1859, concerns how prime numbers are distributed.

**Why it matters:** The verification method matters as much as the result: a Lean proof can be checked by software, quickly and by anyone, rather than by referees over months. The remaining risk sits in the formal statement — if the Lean version drifts from the theorem mathematicians mean, the kernel will cheerfully certify the wrong thing. ([Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/))

## A preprint puts the matrix multiplication exponent at 9/4

A preprint posted November 24 claims the matrix multiplication exponent, written omega, is 9/4. That exponent measures how the cost of multiplying matrices grows with their size; the theoretical floor is 2, and 9/4 is 2.25. The paper appears in OpenAI's mathematics repository and has not been refereed.

**Why it matters:** Omega is one of the most studied constants in algorithms, because a long list of problems can be reduced to matrix multiplication, so a lower bound shifts what theorists think is achievable. These results are asymptotic and will not speed up ordinary matrix code at realistic sizes, but a claim at this level is exactly the kind that takes experts weeks to check. ([The matrix multiplication exponent is nine fourths [pdf]](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-matrix-multiplication-exponent-is-nine-fourths-November-24-2026/paper.pdf))

## Inkling 2 Mini is sized for one workstation GPU

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini on November 20 as a small model you run yourself rather than only through an API. Coverage since has treated it that way, including a walkthrough of tuning inference on a single RTX PRO 6000 workstation card.

**Why it matters:** A model that fits on one workstation card changes who can experiment: no per-token bill, no data leaving the building, and latency you can predict. The cost moves to operations — quantization, memory, and throughput become yours to tune — which is why local-inference guides tend to appear within days of a release. ([Inkling 2 Mini](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/))

## Anthropic's 2028 power mix: mostly gas, a little coal

An outside analysis of how AI datacenters are powered projects that 87% of Anthropic's electricity will come from natural gas and 11% from coal in 2028. That leaves only a few percent for every other source combined.

**Why it matters:** AI's environmental footprint is increasingly an electricity story, and gas plants are among the fastest new capacity to build, so near-term growth in compute tends to raise emissions even where companies sign clean-power deals. When comparing providers on sustainability, the useful question is which source is actually running the equipment, not only how much renewable energy was contracted. ([87% of the Anthropic power will come from gas and 11% from coal in 2028](https://betterdatacenterproject.com/2026/11/16/how-ai-is-powered-a-breakdown-of-byop-and-grid-power/))
